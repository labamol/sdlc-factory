# Customer Feedback Portal — Business Requirements

## Feedback Submission

- Customers must be able to submit feedback with a category and free-text comment.
- The system shall acknowledge every submission with a unique reference number.
- Submissions must be stored durably and survive service restarts.

## Feedback Review

- Support staff shall be able to list and filter feedback by category and date.
- The review dashboard should feel fast and user-friendly for support staff.
- Closed feedback must be retained for a period that is currently TBD pending compliance sign-off.

## Reporting

- Managers must receive a weekly summary report of feedback volume by category.
- The report delivery channel must be chosen; email vs. dashboard is a pending decision.
